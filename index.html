Good to know

How long does delivery take?

Orders are delivered within 3–5 working days. We confirm your order on WhatsApp and share tracking details once it ships.

Is there a warranty?

Every watch includes a 1-year warranty. If your watch has a manufacturing defect we will repair or replace it.

Can I pay on delivery?

Yes. Cash on delivery is available. Bank transfer, JazzCash and Easypaisa are also supported.

Do the watches work with iPhone and Android?

Yes. All NEXWATCH models connect to both iOS and Android through the companion app.

Can I return a watch?

You can return an unused watch in its original packaging within 7 days of delivery. Message us on WhatsApp to start a return.

How do I track my order?

After you send your order on WhatsApp we reply with a confirmation and keep you updated until your watch arrives.
